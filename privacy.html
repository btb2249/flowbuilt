Privacy Policy — FlowBuilt

Last updated: November 24, 2026

FlowBuilt is made by Bethel Ventures. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. FlowBuilt has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, share, or send your personal information anywhere — the app collects no personal data at all.

How the app works

  • FlowBuilt is a back-office tool for a hardscape business: instant estimates, an estimate tracker, project records, and invoices — all created and stored right on your phone.
  • The app's price book is built in and editable by you in the app. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere — there is no server for it to go to.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your estimates, projects, invoices, and prices are stored in a private database on your phone only.
  • When you delete a record, it is gone. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Phone calls

  • The app has tap-to-call buttons. Tapping one opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in — the call itself happens in your normal phone app, like any other call.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take payments or ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.

Children: FlowBuilt is a business tool made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
